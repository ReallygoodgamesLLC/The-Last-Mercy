The Last Mercy

You have a sword and the wreck of your ship. Every other ship out there has a crew. Take theirs.

At sea

W/S raise / lower sails · A/D steer
Q fire port (left) · E fire starboard (right)
F board, dock or go ashore · R repair with timber · M sea chart
Drag to look around

Boarding

WASD move · Space or click to strike
Shift dodge · watch for raised swords
Beat the crew, then their captain

The trick

You start shipwrecked. Build a raft from the wreckage, reach a ship at anchor and steal her, then use her guns: cannon fire kills crew, but sink a ship and you lose her maps, men and hull. Cannonballs hit whatever they reach, including your own fleet. Rival gangs sail in packs: take the leader to break them.

On a phone

Left stick steers and moves. Buttons fire each side, strike, dodge, repair and act. Drag the sea to look.
